## Compensation Policy

1. Our organization is committed to providing equal, market-competitive compensation based on role responsibilities, skill levels, and annual performance evaluations.
2. Base salaries are benchmarked annually against industry standards, with merit-based increases awarded during the annual review cycle based on performance and budget.
3. Overtime limits are capped at **up to 3 hours per week** for contract-based employees, and **up to 10 hours per week** for B2B contractors.
4. Promotion into a higher job tier triggers a compensation review to align pay with the new role’s salary band, while lateral transfers generally retain current base pay.
5. All compensation details remain strictly confidential and are audited periodically by Human Resources to maintain internal equity and compliance.
6. The company celebrates long-term tenure with milestone anniversary bonuses: **$500** for 5 years, **$1,000** for 10 years, **$2,000** for 15 years, and **$3,500** for 20 years of continuous service.
7. Employees who become new parents through birth or adoption receive a one-time cash bonus equivalent to **1 full month of their base salary**.
8. To encourage physical wellness, the company reimburses sports and gym memberships up to **$100 per month**.
9. Gym reimbursement requires proof of payment showing the charge was paid directly from the employee’s personal bank account or credit card; third-party payments are ineligible.
