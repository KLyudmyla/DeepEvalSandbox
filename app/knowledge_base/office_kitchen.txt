Office Kitchen Rules

The office kitchen is available to all employees during normal office hours.
Employees should clean their dishes after eating or preparing food.
Food should be stored only in the designated refrigerator areas.
Employees should label food that they keep in the shared refrigerator.
The kitchen is cleared every Friday at 4 PM.
Employees must remove their food from the refrigerator before the weekly cleaning.
Unlabeled or expired food may be discarded during the Friday cleaning.
Do not leave food on tables or kitchen counters overnight.
Employees should clean spills immediately after they occur.
The microwave should be cleaned after heating food that causes spills or strong odors.
Personal dishes should not be left in the sink.
The kitchen is not intended for long-term storage of personal food.
Employees should report broken kitchen equipment to the office administration team.
Please respect other employees by keeping noise to a reasonable level.
Coffee, tea, and other shared supplies should be used responsibly.
